01 / RESEARCH

Making LUMEN more dependable.

I’m exploring a parallel vision pipeline for accessible navigation: semantic segmentation for scene context, object detectors for moving hazards, and a scoring layer to prioritise what a user needs to know. The design question continues beyond detection: how can audio and haptic feedback help without adding noise?
